# Continuity evaluation requests

The same requests are provided independently to the baseline and candidate evaluator. They receive only the skill and these raw requests, without intended answers or the product critique. Each case is independent.

## 1 — First conversation

I'm coaching tee-ball for the first time. The league calls it 6U. I have a box of gear and probably about ten kids. Practice is next week. I honestly don't know what you need from me. Can you help me get started? Don't give me a giant checklist.

## 2 — Next practice

Next practice is 40 minutes for the same eight beginner six-year-olds with me and one approved assistant. We have gloves, six soft balls, cones and flat bases; skip batting. We can set up 10 minutes early. We'll use the same large grass area: two independent rolling lanes fit, but the longer throw-and-return route doesn't. League coverage, pickup and restroom arrangements remain in place; we'll check today's conditions before play. Last time the straight adult rolls worked well, the long throwing line lost them, and they loved Baseball Statues. Make the next plan, and give me a small note I can paste into a new chat next week so I don't have to reconstruct all of this.

## 3 — Changed staffing

I pasted the note from our last practice below. Now I have twelve kids instead of eight, my assistant is away, and I'm not sure whether the league allows me to run it alone. Can you update it for tomorrow? I don't have time to fill in everything again.
Previous note: 45 minutes; eight beginners aged six; two approved adults; two independent grass lanes; gloves, soft balls, cones and flat bases; no batting; league coverage and care arrangements confirmed for two adults. Straight rolls went well; keep the fun final game. A private parent note says [fictional child name] has [private medical detail]. Next goal: move behind a gentle roll.

## 4 — Small request

Please write just a three-sentence parent reminder to bring water and arrive ten minutes early for [practice date]. Pickup stays at [time]. We already have our plan; no drills or planning questions, please.

## 5 — Unplayed plan (additional counterexample)

Last week's practice was cancelled before anyone arrived. The plan was 35 minutes for seven beginning 6U players, two approved adults, soft balls and cones, with straight rolls, an optional small side roll and Baseball Statues. None of it happened. Next time the field slot is 25 minutes; the same two adults have confirmed, and the league's pickup/restroom coverage is unchanged. The site and conditions still need checking. Please give me only a short note I can paste into a new chat later. I don't need the new practice plan yet, and I have no observations about what worked.
