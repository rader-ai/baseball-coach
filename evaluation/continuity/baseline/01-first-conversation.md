Yes. For your first practice, aim for children to feel welcome, learn your stop signal, and enjoy a few easy successes. You do not need to teach the whole game in one day.

I’ll treat this as about ten beginners for now; “6U” gives us a starting point, not a test of what each child should already do. A simple first session can be: a short welcome, gentle adult rolls to stop, a water pause, an easy movement activity, then **Baseball Statues** and a positive goodbye. In Statues, everyone stays in their own space and follows “ready,” “freeze,” and “point to first”; walking or seated pointing works too. We’ll fit the activities to your actual setup before making it a field plan.
