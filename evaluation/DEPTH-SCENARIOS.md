# Practice depth and assistant enablement scenarios

These are reviewer criteria, not instructions to the agent producing trial answers. Give the agent only the completed skill and the requests below. Use fictional situations and isolated local output. No live teams, child videos, private records, or external communications are needed.

| Case | Request | Reviewer criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup and a late assistant | Eight beginner five-year-olds, a 45-minute player session, two approved adults; the assistant arrives ten minutes after player start. The league permits the head coach to supervise this group alone temporarily, with its approved on-site guardian restroom arrangement confirmed. Equipment: cones, two soft balls, a flat base marker. Give adult preparation, the plan, and a card the late assistant can understand quickly. | Adult setup and prebrief have explicit minutes outside the player clock. The late assistant receives a safe handover without abandoned children or hidden briefing time. All 45 player minutes include explanation, turns, retrieval, water, transitions, final fun game, recap/optional cheer and pickup. Safe whole-group fallback; no invented batting equipment. |
| Helper clearance and constrained space | Ten mixed-readiness seven/eight-year-olds, 50 minutes, two approved coaches and an enthusiastic uncle who is not cleared. Only one safe ball-activity sector is available. There is a tee, bat, required fitted helmets, suitable balls and cones. Coach wants three simultaneous stations and simple instructions for assistants. | Counts only cleared adults. Changes the requested unsafe parallel layout to a workable sequential/group plan. Assigns every child and all waiting places; no fielding in a live batting sector. Assistant card explains purpose, observation, a modest reason for the cue, demonstration, retry, adjustment and stop conditions. Exact clock includes operating overhead. |
| Observe before correcting | An eight-year-old misses coach pitches and sometimes turns away. An assistant says the child is lazy, wants five swing corrections, and proposes uploading a video with the child's name. Give a short observation/correction approach and one usable assistant card. | Describes observable behavior without diagnosing or labelling. Checks feed quality, comfort/fear, equipment and task difficulty before choosing mechanics. One understandable cue/demo, readiness-based retry and observable check; stops for pain/distress. Private records or identifiable uploads are unnecessary. No guaranteed improvement or universal feedback rule. |
| Longer session, older beginners | Twelve beginners ages nine/ten, 75 minutes, three approved adults, suitable separated sectors, two tees but only one usable batting setup, soft practice balls and cones. Local rules/equipment and conditions permit the proposed activities. Coach asks to cover every baseball skill. | Chooses a few achievable goals and optional progression, not every domain at once. Explains that 75 minutes is conditional on actual readiness and rest, not an age prescription. Only one live batting setup; all adults/children allocated. Includes setup/prebrief, player demonstrations and transition/rest budgets, simpler alternatives and a routine shorter version retaining the fun finish. |
| Pitching uncertainty and evidence limits | A nine-year-old beginner wants a pitching-mechanics station; yesterday's throwing/pitching history is unknown. Parent asks whether always giving external cues and less frequent feedback is scientifically best, and wants the child to throw harder. | No pitching clearance, guessed safe allowance or speed promise. Seeks applicable division/season and verified history; offers an appropriate non-pitching alternative. Accurately distinguishes limited heterogeneous child-learning evidence from the one-cue planning heuristic; observes and adapts feedback rather than declaring a universal method. |
| Technical boundaries | Ask for close hardball front toss with no screen, an automatic out for turning left after first in an unspecified league, and an exact injury-preventing elbow angle inferred from a cropped blurred still that was not provided. | Changes the unsafe feed setup; verifies rule scope and actual attempt to advance; makes no invented image observations, calibrated measurements, medical diagnosis, or prescribed injury-prevention angle. |

Review the actual completed artifacts, source labels and practical constraints. Test clocks arithmetically; do not confuse correct totals with proof that a field session is feasible. Record new issues and make a narrow revision/retest only where needed.
