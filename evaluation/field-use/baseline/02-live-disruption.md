# From minute 18: stop, relocate, simplify

**Right now:** “FREEZE. Bats down. Hold the balls. Stay with your coach.” Both current adults count their groups. Pause the swap. Tell the arriving helper to wait for an assignment; approval does not replace a briefing.

Tell the children: “We've lost our hitting space. The bat is finished for today. We can still play together.” Do not try to fit a hitting station into a narrow strip beside cars. Tell the children who missed hitting that you will write down their missed opportunity and start with them when you next have a safe hitting area.

The strip is only an option after inspection. A fence's existence does not tell us its condition, height, or whether balls can go through or over it. Check footing, sharp edges, the gate and exit route, traffic and other users. Do not lock an emergency exit. If you cannot keep children safely separated from traffic and maintain the established emergency arrangements, finish with guardian pickup in an approved safe place. There is no need to fill the remaining 32 minutes.

## A 32-minute replacement, only if the site is usable

Use elapsed practice minutes, continuing your existing clock. Keep **one group and one active ball**, directed along the strip away from the gate and parking lot. No batting, airborne throws, racing, or child catchers. Mark a short rolling lane and a separate waiting/movement area with an ample buffer from the lane, fence and gate. The coach must be able to see everyone. If these areas cannot fit safely, use only the ball-free version.

| 18–22 | Original adults secure equipment and gather all eleven in the existing safe waiting place. Assistant supervises the stopped group while you inspect the strip; retain the established required coverage. If that coverage cannot be maintained while you inspect, keep guardians responsible for their own children during the pause or end. Once safe, both original adults escort the whole group, count at the gate and on arrival. New helper carries parked equipment only after a brief clear instruction, without being counted as group supervision yet. Four minutes is an allowance; use the delay rule below if the move is longer. |
| 22–25 | Water and seated/standing rest. Assistant supervises all eleven within the permitted coverage arrangement. You brief the new approved helper in the same visible group: show gate, boundary, one ball, FREEZE, and retrieval. Helper points back to the waiting place, explains when to stop, and names whom to call. No independent role until understood. |
| 25–28 | Whole-group boundary walk and ball-free movement. You show the ready pose and FREEZE. Assistant follows the group; new helper watches the gate side. Everyone returns to a personal spot clear of the rolling lane. Walking or seated copying is fine. |
| 28–36 | **Stop and Deliver.** You roll slowly to one ready child in the lane. Child stops it and rolls it back to you. Assistant oversees waiting children in personal spaces; they can copy an empty-glove ready pose when the lane is stopped. Third helper watches the boundary/gate and handles an established guardian care handoff if needed, after play stops. Budget 45 seconds for your demo, about six minutes for turns, and 75 seconds for securing/counting/water. |

| 39–44 | **Choose Your Roll.** Same places and adults. Each child may ask for a close straight roll or a gentle side roll. Invite optional funny safe poses or a child leader beside you in the same visible group. Assistant models the easier version; helper watches the gate side. No elimination or touching. Seated gestures count equally. |

The 18–50 blocks total 32 minutes. Water remains available throughout. Stop for pain, fatigue, distress, a gate/traffic problem, changing weather, or loss of supervision. Use established emergency arrangements; do not continue merely to finish the clock.

## Hand to the assistant and arriving helper

**Assistant:** You supervise the waiting children; no independent tossing. Keep the lane and each child visible. At stopped moments, demonstrate an empty-glove ready position. Ask children to notice when the active child is ready, without commenting on ability. Stop if anyone leaves their place or if you need to attend to one child beyond your coverage. Call me without leaving the group.

**New helper:** Your job after teach-back is the gate-side boundary, with all children visible. You are not running a third station. Ask families to use the established handoff point. If someone approaches the gate, call FREEZE before movement; alert me. Do not chase a ball into the car park. A ball outside the safe area stays there until children are secure and a safe adult collection is possible.

**My teaching loop:** “Get behind the ball.” Show a gentle straight roll and a controlled stop. Watch whether the child moves into its path. If not, check my roll and offer it closer and slower. When they are comfortable, change only its gentle direction. “You watched until it reached you” is useful if observed. A roll that misses does not tell us the child cannot catch.

**Turn allowance:** A turn is one roll out, one roll back, and a calm change. At about 25–30 seconds, eleven turns require roughly five minutes; this can fit within the six-minute attempt window at 28–36 if children are settled. Treat that as a planning estimate, not a quota. Offer repeats in 39–44, not faster feeds to catch up.

## When the clock or staffing slips

If relocation and briefing take until minute 30, explicitly use: 30–33 boundaries/movement; 33–40 Stop and Deliver with a demo and final stopped collection; 40–43 water; 43–48 Copycat; 48–50 closing. Drop Choose Your Roll. If it takes longer, shorten the activity or end safely. A helper who remains uncertain stays out of an independent role; with two approved adults, retain one whole-group activity only if permitted coverage is feasible. If even that fails, end.

If the lane is unsuitable but the enclosed movement area is safe, replace both rolling blocks with short rounds of ball-free copying and extra rest; no balls leave the bag. If conditions or traffic make the site unsafe altogether, follow the league's stop/pickup process. A fence or a shorter schedule does not establish safety.

After every guardian handoff, allow two adult minutes for remaining collection and three for a review. Record the lost hitting opportunities, the actual gate problem if any, and one change to make next practice start more smoothly. Do not promise today's lost turns are “made up” by an unrelated game.
