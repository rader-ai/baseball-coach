# Minute 21: remaining field card

**Now:** Keep equipment secured; count nine into one visible waiting group. You supervise everyone; no second station. Resume batting only if you can see all nine while tending the tee, with waiting children outside full bat reach and ball paths, an empty hitting sector, and correctly fitted required helmets confirmed. Otherwise keep the bat parked and substitute ball-free ready/freeze poses for that block.

| Minute | Do this |
|---|---|
| **21–24** | Move together, inspect the single activity area, explain waiting places, and rehearse “FREEZE—hold equipment; look at me.” Budget all relocation and briefing here. |
| **24–32** | Offer the **four who haven't hit** one tee turn each. Allow 90 seconds per complete turn, plus two minutes for demonstration/closure. Cue: “Send the ball into that space.” Adjust tee for easier contact. Only called batter holds bat; you stay outside swing reach. Others watch from their waiting place. Park bat before any retrieval; retrieve only within the visible cleared sector. Leave distant balls. No child catcher. |
| **32–34** | Secure bat, water, count. Water remains available throughout. |
| **34–41** | One child at a time receives your gentle ground roll and rolls back. Offer everyone a closer roll or watching first, especially the two uneasy catchers; no forced airborne catches. Cue: “Get behind it.” Nine 35-second cycles plus demo/margin fit one round. |
| **41–46** | **Baseball Statues:** equipment secured, everyone in spaced spots. Call ready, freeze, or silly safe pose; demonstrate first. No elimination. Easier: seated pose or pointing. |
| **46–48** | Specific appreciation, optional recap, supervised handoffs; stay until all collected. |

If setup runs long, drop batting; note missed hitters for first turns next practice. For care, stop everyone and use the established own-guardian handoff without leaving. If coverage, visibility, or safety fails, end play with supervised pickup.
