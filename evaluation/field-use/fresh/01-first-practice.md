# First tee-ball practice: 38-minute field card

**Eight children, ages 5–6; coach + approved assistant. Two things today:** stop a slow rolling ball; run through first. This is an introduction, with several easy retries. Batting can have its own focus another day.

**Places and equipment:** Coach keeps four children in the rolling area; assistant keeps four in the separate running area. Open both only after checking that stray rolls cannot enter the running or waiting paths. Use three cones per area: one waiting marker and two marking a wide target gate. At running, the gate represents first base: run between the cones, never onto them. Leave clear slowing space beyond and a separate walk-back route outside the running lane. Adults stand where active and waiting children remain visible. Waiting children stay beside their marker, outside all paths. Put water beside each waiting area. Use gloves and one soft ball; secure seven spare balls, tee, bat, and helmets at the equipment spot.

**Stop signal:** “FREEZE—hold the ball; look at me.” Anyone may call it. Nobody throws, rolls, runs, or collects until directed. Coach authorizes restarts. Water is available throughout; use the confirmed guardian/restroom and emergency procedures. Stop for pain, fatigue, distress, unsafe space, or lost coverage; follow current conditions and league policy for shortening or cancellation.

| Elapsed time | What to do |
|---|---|
| **0–3** | Both adults accept handoffs, count eight, introduce names, point out water/waiting places, and rehearse FREEZE. Walk children to their adult. |
| **3–15** | **Coach: roll and stop.** Demonstrate briefly: “Get behind the ball.” Roll gently to one ready child through the gate; child stops it, then rolls it back when you are ready. Next child enters only after the ball is controlled. Easier: closer, slower, straight roll; later offer one gentle side roll. **Assistant: run through first**, following her card. Both rotate all four; waiting children watch one action, without balls. Include demos, missed-ball recovery, encouragement, and stopped collection here. |
| **15–18** | FREEZE; coach secures the ball. Water, count each group, then adults escort groups along clear routes to swap. Confirm both groups ready before restarting. |
| **18–30** | Repeat the two activities with the other four. Begin with the same short demonstrations. Offer an easier retry before adding challenge. |
| **30–32** | Secure ball and gloves, drink, count eight, and join in one clear area. Adults show spaced personal spots. |
| **32–36** | **Final fun game: Baseball Statues.** Coach demonstrates and calls “ready,” “slow-motion run,” or “freeze.” Everyone poses or moves feet in place; no traveling or elimination. Children may suggest a safe silly statue. Assistant watches spacing and joins the demonstration. Easier: seated poses or pointing. All bats and balls stay secured. |
| **36–38** | Optional question: “What helped you stop the ball?” Name one action you actually saw, offer a wave/cheer, and begin supervised guardian handoffs. |

**Total: 38 minutes.** Supervision continues until every handoff is complete. If separation fails, use the sequential fallback below; if approved coverage fails, stop and retain supervision through safe pickup.

## Detachable assistant card

**Your job:** Four children at running, **3–15 and 18–30**. Teach running through first; you are the only cue-giver here.

**Places:** One runner; three beside the waiting cone, outside the lane and separate return path. Keep all four visible. No balls or bats. The two-cone gate is first; leave clear space beyond it.

**Show:** Walk between the cones, continue beyond them, slow, then walk back outside the lane. Cue: **“Run past first, then slow down.”** This directs attention beyond the base. Send the next child only once the route is clear.

**Watch → retry:** If a child stops at the gate, walk the route together in view of the group, then offer another try. Easier: walk or shorten the approach. Praise an observed controlled finish.

**Turns:** Aim for four or five complete routes each, roughly two minutes between starts. Never race or hurry returns.

**Stop/change:** Call FREEZE for danger, care needs, distress, or uncertainty. Keep your group; call coach. Count and escort children only on coach’s swap signal. Teach back these instructions before intake.

## Preparation notes — outside the player clock

**Use the ten minutes beforehand:**

- **−10 to −6:** Coach inspects both grass areas and marks rolling/waiting geometry. Assistant checks equipment, water access, running/return routes, and guardian boundary. Together verify separation, footing, current local conditions, and applicable league directions. Do not accept children yet; early arrivals stay with guardians.
- **−6 to −2:** Walk the actual areas together. State the two goals and adult assignments; demonstrate the cue, easier retry, FREEZE, collection, and escorted swap. Assistant teaches back her assignment and stop procedure. Confirm how either adult summons help while retaining children.
- **−2 to 0:** Resolve uncertainties, park unused gear, check emergency access and readiness, then open intake. Coverage, emergency, and guardian/restroom processes are already league-confirmed; use them.

**Opportunity check:** In each 12-minute block, allow three minutes for demonstration, encouragement, recovery, and interruption margin, leaving nine minutes. One adult-led rolling lane, a conservative 25-second complete roll–stop–return–change cycle, and four children gives about **five attempts each**, roughly **100 seconds between starts** before extra pauses. A 30-second complete running/slowdown/walk-back/change cycle gives **four or five routes each**, roughly **two minutes between starts**. These support trying, simplifying, and retrying today's two actions; watching is not counted as an attempt. Check the first actual cycles. If slower, shorten the roll or running route safely, retain easy retries, and accept fewer turns without rushing. The final game is simultaneous, so all eight participate within its four minutes.

**If the areas cannot be separated:** Keep all eight together with both adults. Preserve the clock: **3–15** roll-and-stop with coach feeding and assistant supervising the visible waiting group; **18–30** ball-free running with assistant sending runners and coach supervising waiting/return. Expect about two rolling attempts and two running routes each; explicitly narrow the goal to a first introduction. Use the same transitions, game, and closing. Do not add an unverified second lane.

**If setup overruns:** Guardians retain care until safe setup is complete. Charge lost minutes to **3–15**, keep the scheduled water, game, and closing, and reduce the first activity's repetitions. Postpone if safe setup and approved coverage cannot be achieved. If the assistant is absent, do not infer solo permission from the confirmed two-adult arrangement; delay intake pending approved coverage.

**Late arrival:** Guardian retains care until the stopped **15–18** pause. Accept handoff and demonstrate boundaries/FREEZE then; if more time is needed, take it explicitly from **18–30**. Keep both groups stopped until ready.

**After every pickup:** Allow two minutes for shared teardown, then three for an adult review: one observed success, one waiting/setup difficulty, one adjustment. Extend teardown if needed. These are planning suggestions, not a verified local curriculum; current local weather, site layout, and equipment rules remain checks for the adults on site.
