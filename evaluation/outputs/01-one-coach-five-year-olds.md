Here is a 35-minute, one-group practice for eight five-year-olds. The goals are stopping a rolling ball and running through a finish line. With one coach and the equipment listed, keep the bat and tee stored for this session; batting would add equipment and supervision needs that are not established here.

Before starting, confirm the league-approved restroom arrangement. Solo group supervision permission does not establish permission to leave seven children while helping one. For this plan, each child's guardian must remain available on site to take their own child to the restroom, using the league's handoff procedure. Confirm that arrangement with the league and guardians before accepting children. If it cannot be arranged, obtain approved coverage or postpone; the schedule below cannot solve missing supervision.

Assumptions: clear, level space away from other games and traffic; suitable weather under current league policy; water at the visible waiting area; no independent stations. Inspect the space during arrival. If weather, footing, or the ability to keep all eight together makes this unsuitable, shorten or stop.

Use two cones to mark a broad waiting line beside the activity lane, outside its ball path. The other two mark a start and finish, roughly 15 feet apart, with room beyond to slow down. Children run beside the finish cone, never onto it. Point out a separate walk-back route that does not cross the lane. Keep the water, three spare soft balls, tee, and bat at the coach's equipment spot out of children's access. Only one ball is live.

Teach one stop signal: **“Freeze—ball down, eyes here.”** On that signal everyone stops, including the runner. No rolling, throwing, or retrieving until you restart. Nobody chases a missed ball; park the other balls and walk the whole group to collect it if you cannot retrieve it while maintaining supervision.

| Clock | Run it this way | Cue and adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 minutes | Gather and count all eight, show the waiting line and water, demonstrate the stop signal, and walk the run/walk-back route together. All equipment stays parked. | “Freeze means stop.” Rehearse twice if needed. |
| 4–8 | Whole-group movement in personal spaces: march, reach, then bend into a ready stance. You face all eight. Include a brief demonstration and settle back at the waiting line. | “Bend your knees.” Easier: stay standing and copy just the hands. |
| 8–16 | Roll and stop. Call one child into the lane, roll gently from a few feet away, and have them stop and roll the ball back. Aim for two turns each, with a short demonstration included. Others stay at the waiting line and watch for the ball stopping. | “Get behind the ball.” Easier: roll closer and slower. Harder: roll slightly to one side only after straight rolls work. |
| 16–19 | Everyone takes water together within your view; park the ball and prepare the run lane. | Water is also available whenever needed; pause the activity if helping someone would divide your attention. |
| 19–26 | One child at a time goes from the start, through the finish, slows down, and walks back on the separate route. Release the next child only after the previous child is back. Include a demonstration, then one or two turns each as time allows. | “Run past the cone.” Easier: walk the entire route. A child can watch and call “through!” instead. |
| 26–32 | Return to roll and stop, one ball and one child at a time. Give each child one turn first, then repeat if time remains. Include the regroup and demonstration. | “Get behind the ball.” Offer the same slower, closer roll; do not add a throw. |
| 32–35 | Park equipment, gather and count, ask “What does freeze mean?”, thank the group, and begin supervised guardian handoffs. If a guardian is late, supervision continues after minute 35. | A wave or quiet goodbye is fine. |

**If a child needs the restroom:** stop immediately, secure the ball and bat, gather and count the group, and call that child's on-site guardian to you. Use the approved handoff, keep the other children with you, and resume only after the handoff and recount. The child can rejoin after the guardian brings them back. Do not send them alone, assign another child as escort, or leave the group to escort them yourself. If the guardian is unavailable or the arrangement fails, stop the session and activate the league's approved coverage/pickup procedure while retaining supervision. Never require a child to wait until the scheduled break; cut activities to accommodate the interruption, keeping the planned finish time.
