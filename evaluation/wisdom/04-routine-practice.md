# Twenty minutes for six beginners

**Goal:** stop a rolling ball and roll toward a target. Use the six supplied softballs and four cones only. No gloves, bats, bases, or tee are needed. Balls stay on the ground throughout.

**Assumptions:** the two adults are approved, league staffing permission is established, and space, weather, and field conditions are suitable as stated. Drinking water and the league's usual care and pickup arrangements must remain available; the equipment list covers activity equipment. These are practice suggestions, not a league curriculum.

**Setup and supervision:** Adult A has three children; Adult B has three. Make two well-separated rolling areas with the same orientation and ample empty space beyond them. Each area gets two cones and one live ball. Place the other four balls together in an adult-controlled equipment spot outside travel routes. Point out each group's waiting place to the side, outside the lane and possible missed rolls. Each adult watches their entire group. If balls or children could enter the other group's area, use one whole-group lane instead: A leads; B supervises the waiting group.

**Stop signal:** “Freeze—hold every ball.” Rehearse it. All balls must be held before anyone collects, changes position, or crosses a lane. Adults release one child at a time to retrieve a missed ball; everyone else stays in place. No independent ball play while waiting. There is no batting area in this session and no swinging.

| Elapsed time | Activity, cue, and adjustment |
|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | **Welcome and setup.** B keeps all six children together in the waiting area while A places cones and parks unused balls. Both adults show boundaries and rehearse the stop signal. Split into the two groups and walk to positions with balls held. |
| 2:00–4:00 | **Ready-body warm-up.** With balls parked, each adult demonstrates a balanced stance. Children copy in their own spaces, then take one small side-step and reset. Cue: “Get ready.” Easier: remain still or use a comfortable seated ready position. |
| 4:00–9:00 | **Roll and stop.** Each adult rolls gently toward one ready child; the other two wait at their designated side spot and watch the ball. Cue: “Get behind it.” Child stops the ball, then gently rolls it back only after the adult says ready. Use a brief turn, then freeze and switch. Easier: a slower, closer roll; if comfortable, add a small sideways roll. No extra speed. Missed-ball retrieval and all switches are included in these five minutes. |
| 9:00–10:00 | **Water and reset.** Adults hold the two live balls and keep their three children supervised during the break. Set each pair of cones as a wide gate in the empty target end of its area; briefly demonstrate the next activity. Water is also available whenever needed. |
| 10:00–15:00 | **Roll through the gate.** One child at a time rolls one ball toward the two-cone gate; nobody stands beyond it. Cue: “Roll toward the middle.” Easier: move closer or widen the gate. When comfortable, narrow the gate a little without also increasing distance. Freeze; the adult confirms the whole area is stopped, then permits that child to walk out, collect the ball, and return. Next child goes only after the lane is clear. The adult stays where all three children remain visible. Waiting children can point toward the middle, with no balls in hand. Include a final freeze and return both live balls to the equipment spot by 15:00. |
| 15:00–19:00 | **Final fun game: Baseball Statues.** Balls are secured; children spread out in their personal spaces. A calls “ready,” “freeze,” or “point to first,” demonstrating each. B watches spacing and helps anyone who wants a model. Everyone plays every round; children can suggest one silly stationary pose. Easier: seated poses or pointing. No running, elimination, or penalties. |
| 19:00–20:00 | **Positive wrap-up and handoff.** Invite one optional “What did you try?” Notice a real action, such as waiting until the lane was clear. A manages guardian handoffs under the league's procedure; B keeps children still awaiting pickup together and supervised. |

**Total: 20 minutes**, including explanation, collection, water, transitions, the final game, and closing. Supervision continues after 20:00 until every required handoff is complete.

If a child needs care or an adult must leave, stop all activity first, secure balls, and use the league's approved coverage/guardian procedure. Do not assume the remaining adult may continue alone; resume only with permitted, workable supervision. If conditions worsen, follow league policy and shorten, postpone, or cancel. For a routine time loss, shorten the middle drills and preserve a short Statues game; safety and fatigue take precedence. Stop any activity that causes pain.
