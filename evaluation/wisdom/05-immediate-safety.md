# Stop the throwing-lane crossing now

**Say loudly and plainly:** “STOP! Hold every ball. No throwing. Nobody cross.”

Get the throwers stopped immediately. Let any ball already in flight finish; then direct children to the designated waiting area outside the lane. Check that every ball is controlled before anybody collects or crosses. Do not start with a joke or a long comparison while balls are moving.

Keep the drill stopped while you fix the route: show where children wait, where they walk, and who gives permission to cross. Put an approved adult in charge of the crossing point if staffing allows. Otherwise run one turn at a time and keep the whole group together. If you cannot supervise the lane and the route safely, replace throwing with a ball-free activity.

Rehearse once without throws: “Show me where you wait.” Then: “Show me what you do when I say stop.” Resume only when the lane is empty, receivers are ready, and an adult controls the next throw. Crossing or retrieval happens only after a stop and confirmation that every ball is held.

After the area is safe, optional brief humor: “The baseballs can wait. They don't have appointments.” Immediately repeat the literal rule: “Cross only after I stop the throws and tell you it's clear.” Save the elaborate coach story for another moment; this instruction needs to be short enough to follow immediately.
