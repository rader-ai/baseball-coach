Here is a **conditional 25-minute plan for tomorrow, October 12**, for ten 7–8-year-olds and two approved coaches. It keeps the work simple, includes substantial shaded rest, and ends active play with a two-minute game.

**What city or ZIP code, practice start time, and current league heat policy apply?** Those missing details determine whether outdoor practice should happen. Before confirming it, check current local conditions and the league's decision process. If that information remains unresolved, postpone the outdoor session. A reduction from 60 to 25 minutes is not heat clearance. Suitable shade and drinking water are prerequisites for this version.

CDC recommends limiting hot-weather outdoor activity, choosing cooler times, pacing activity, and drinking water without waiting for thirst. Weakness or faintness means stopping activity and moving to a cool place; heat-illness symptoms require prompt medical attention. [CDC: Heat and Athletes](https://www.cdc.gov/heat-health/risk-factors/heat-and-athletes.html), checked October 11, 2026. Apply your local policy rather than inventing a universal temperature cutoff.

The coaching goal is **getting ready and stopping a slow ground ball**. Assume one soft ball, four cones, an inspected clear area, and a shaded waiting/rest area alongside it. Check local equipment requirements. This plan has no batting, races, conditioning, or extra repetitions to recover the missing 35 minutes.

Two cones mark the shaded waiting line; two define a short rolling lane directed away from waiting players and other activities. Coach A leads the one active lane. Coach B supervises the waiting group, water, and rest. All ten stay in the same supervised area. Water is available at any time.

“Freeze” means stop and hold the ball. No rolling, throwing, or crossing the lane during retrieval. Nobody chases a missed ball. Coach A retrieves after stopping play, while Coach B retains the waiting group. Bats and spare balls remain secured throughout.

Total: **25 minutes**, including instruction, movement between activities, retrieval, water, game, and closing. Give short, easy attempts rather than a list of corrections. A child can rest without losing their place in the group.

If routinely shortened further for scheduling, trim earlier repetitions and keep a short Baseball Mirror game and goodbye. **Heat symptoms, fatigue, unsafe conditions, or cancellation override the game requirement.** Do not continue activity just to reach the fun ending.

If one coach must provide individual care or leave, stop the activity and gather everyone first. One coach retains the group while the other follows the league's care/guardian procedure. Continue only with permitted, workable supervision; otherwise finish with supervised pickup. Activate the emergency plan promptly for a suspected heat emergency. If a cooler approved time or venue cannot be arranged, postpone rather than improvising a heat exception.
