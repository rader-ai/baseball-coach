Use this as a **35-minute whole-group practice**: stop a rolling ball, then run through a finish. All eight children stay with you; only one child and one ball are active at a time.

Your league has permitted solo group supervision. The remaining prerequisite is its restroom procedure. Before accepting the children, confirm that each child's own guardian will remain available on site for restroom care and that the league permits that handoff. If that arrangement is unavailable, arrange approved coverage or postpone. Solo-supervision permission does not tell us how restroom care is covered.

Assume a clear, level area away from traffic and other games, suitable weather under the league's current policy, and drinking water beside the waiting area. Check the field and required equipment before starting. Use one soft ball and four cones. Keep the three spare balls, tee, and bat secured at your equipment spot. With no protective batting gear established, this session has no batting; available equipment does not all need to be used.

Place two cones to mark a waiting line beside, and well outside, the ball lane. The other two mark a start and finish about 15 feet apart, with space beyond the finish to slow down. Children pass beside the cone, not over it. Point out a separate walk-back route outside the active lane. Position yourself so the active child, waiting line, and water remain visible together. If the site does not allow that, change the layout before beginning.

Teach **“Freeze—hold the ball and look at me.”** All movement stops, including running. No throws, rolls, or lane crossings during retrieval. A missed ball stays where it is until you stop the activity; if you cannot retrieve it while supervising everyone, walk the group with you to collect it. Nobody chases it independently.

| Time | What you do with all eight children | Cue and adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Count children, show the waiting area and water, demonstrate “freeze,” and walk the running and return routes together. Equipment stays parked. | “Stop and look.” Repeat the demonstration if needed. |
| 4–8 | Lead marching, reaching, and ready-position practice in personal spaces. Face the group and keep everyone visible. | “Bend your knees.” Easier: standing or seated hand-position practice. |
| 8–15 | Demonstrate roll-and-stop. One called child enters the lane; you roll gently from close range; they stop the ball and roll it back. Give everyone one turn before repeating. Waiting children watch for the ball to stop. | “Get behind the ball.” Easier: closer, slower roll. Harder: a gentle roll slightly to one side. |
| 15–18 | Park the ball. Take water together, count children, and reset for running. | Water is also available whenever needed; stop the group if helping someone divides your attention. |
| 18–25 | Demonstrate running through the finish and slowing down. One child goes at a time, then walks back on the separate route. Start the next child after the previous child returns. Give all eight a turn before repeats. | “Run past the cone.” Easier: walk the whole route. An observing child may point to the finish. |
| 25–27 | Water and regroup at the waiting line. Recount; bring out the single ball. | No activity during the transition. |
| 27–32 | Repeat roll-and-stop. Each child chooses a straight roll or, if ready, a slight side roll. Include a short reminder and aim for one turn each. | “Get behind the ball.” Freely offer the closer, slower roll again. |
| 32–35 | Secure equipment, gather and count, invite one brief favorite moment with passing allowed, and begin supervised guardian handoffs. | “Thanks for taking turns today.” Supervision continues for every child awaiting pickup after minute 35. |

The blocks total 35 minutes, including demonstrations, transitions, water, retrieval, and closing. Repetitions fit the time available; do not rush children to complete a quota. If waiting becomes difficult, replace the remainder of that block with a whole-group, equipment-free ready-position game rather than creating another station.

**Restroom interruption:** stop immediately, secure the ball and bat, gather and count all eight, and summon the child's on-site guardian to you. Complete the approved handoff while retaining the other seven. Resume only once the group is settled and accounted for. The guardian returns the child directly to you and you update the count. Never escort one child away while leaving the others, send a child alone, or use another child as an escort. If the guardian is unavailable or coverage fails, keep everyone supervised, activate the approved coverage/pickup procedure, and end the activities. Cut later repetitions to preserve the planned finish; do not make the child wait for a scheduled break.

If care needs, weather, field conditions, or behavior make whole-group supervision unworkable, stop and secure equipment first. Use the league's approved support or pickup procedure. Shortening this schedule does not override a cancellation decision. Afterward, note one thing that helped children participate for the next practice.
