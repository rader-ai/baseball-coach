Subject: Welcome to [team] — first practice [confirmed date]

Hi families,

I'm [coach], and our goal is for every child to feel welcome, try new skills, and enjoy baseball. Beginners are welcome.

Our first practice is [date/time] at [location]. Please arrive [confirmed arrival guidance] with [required personal gear], water, and suitable clothing. We provide [confirmed shared gear]; check with us before buying equipment.

Please report absences through [approved adult channel]. Weather updates come from [confirmed decision-maker/channel]. Pickup follows [league procedure]. To discuss an access need or concern, contact [private approved adult channel]; please keep sensitive details out of group messages.

We welcome help with [specific roles], subject to league approval, screening, and training. Thank you for cheering effort and letting the coaches give instructions during play.

[Coach sign-off]
