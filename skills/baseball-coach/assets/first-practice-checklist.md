# First-practice checklist

Before families arrive — suggested 12 adult minutes outside the player clock:

- [ ] Confirm league/division, current rules, approved adults, and safeguarding coverage.
- [ ] Check current weather/field conditions and the league's modification/cancellation policy.
- [ ] Confirm emergency plan, field access, required equipment, and approved pickup/restroom procedures.
- [ ] Choose one or two skills and a timed plan that includes transitions and water.
- [ ] Reserve a simple final fun game using those basics, then a brief positive wrap-up; safety stops override the schedule.
- [ ] First five minutes: head coach inspects/marks lanes; approved assistant checks gear, waiting area, and water/shade. Finish setup before accepting children.
- [ ] Next four minutes: use [assistant cards](assistant-card.md) for a field walkthrough, purpose, one cue/demo, observed difficulty, adjusted retry, and stop/retrieval procedure. Have each helper teach it back.
- [ ] Name one cue-giver per activity and head coach as transition/restart coordinator; anyone can call an immediate stop.
- [ ] Final three minutes: check coverage and readiness for intake. Confirm a budgeted late-player/late-assistant fallback; spectators are not automatically supervisors.
- [ ] Keep private emergency information in the authorized league system.

At arrival:

- [ ] Account for children and show where equipment belongs.
- [ ] Tell caregivers the pickup and weather-update process.
- [ ] Demonstrate “freeze”: stop, hold balls, park bats, look at the coach.
- [ ] Show the first activity; keep the welcome brief.
- [ ] Keep early/late arrivals with their guardians until accepted handoff; introduce late players during a stopped, budgeted intake pause.

At closing:

- [ ] Recognize one act of effort or teamwork; let children pass on speaking.
- [ ] Offer an optional cheer, wave, or quiet acknowledgment after the final game and recap.
- [ ] Collect equipment with all hitting/throwing stopped.
- [ ] Complete supervised guardian handoff and confirm every child is accounted for.
- [ ] Continue supervision after the scheduled finish until every authorized pickup is complete.
- [ ] Only after all handoffs, take three adult minutes: one success, one operational difficulty, one adjustment. Solo coaches can make the same short note.
