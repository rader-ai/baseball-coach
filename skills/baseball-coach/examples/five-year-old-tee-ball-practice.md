# Five-year-old tee-ball practice: 32 minutes with one adult

## Field card

**Six beginners; one approved adult.** Run this abbreviated introduction only if solo supervision and a specific guardian/care/restroom arrangement are permitted and feasible. Otherwise obtain approved coverage or postpone. Goal: learn FREEZE, try stopping a gentle roll, and find first—not a high-repetition skill session.

**Gear/places:** one soft ball, cones, flat first-base marker, water. Keep all six visible. Mark one rolling lane, waiting places outside it, and a separate running return route. No bats. One child/ball is active; waiting children may copy a ready pose without equipment in safe personal spaces.

**Say:** “FREEZE: stop, hold the ball, look at me.” Demonstrate. Close the lane before collection or movement. Stop for pain, distress, wandering, weather concerns, or lost coverage. For care/restroom needs, stop the whole group and use the agreed guardian procedure; never leave children or send one alone.

| Time | Whole group: coach leads and supervises everyone |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Accept handoffs, show places and practice FREEZE. |
| 4–6 | Everyone marches/reaches in personal spaces, then makes a ready pose. Walking/seated versions welcome. |
| 6–14 | Show a slow roll for 30 seconds. Call one ready child, roll gently; child stops and rolls back, then changes. “Body behind it.” Offer closer/straight first, gentle side roll only when ready. Reserve the final minute for stopped collection/count and allow pauses. |
| 14–17 | Water, ball secured, count. Late-child intake only while stopped; guardian retains care until handoff. |
| 17–23 | Walk the first-base route for one minute. Then one child at a time runs through first and walks the separate return. “Through the base.” Walk instead if preferred. Close/count in the final 30 seconds. |
| 23–25 | Water; secure ball and spread into personal spaces. |
| 25–29 | **Final fun game: Baseball Statues.** Show “ready,” “freeze,” and “point to first”; everyone copies each call together. Invite silly safe poses. Seated gestures count; nobody is out. No ball or bat is active. |
| 29–32 | Optional favorite moment, one real appreciation, optional wave/cheer, then begin supervised pickup. |

Total: 32 minutes. Water remains available throughout. If the coach cannot see both the runner and waiting group, replace 17–23 with whole-group base-pointing and ready poses. If intake needs extra instruction, use the beginning of 17–23 and shorten running, not needed water. The game never overrides a safety stop. Continue supervision through every authorized pickup.

## Preparation notes — read before the session

**Adult time:** T−10 to T−5 inspect/mark the small familiar setup; T−5 to T−2 rehearse cue/stop/collection and confirm care coverage; T−2 to T0 check conditions and intake readiness. Children remain with guardians until accepted. Increase preparation time if needed; don't leave accepted children to finish setup. This short duration is a deliberate option, not an official age standard.

**Opportunity check:** the eight-minute rolling block allows about six usable attempt minutes after demo, closure, and margin. At a conservative 30-second roll/return/change, about twelve cycles permit roughly two tries each, with around three minutes between the same child's turn starts within this single visit. There is no later ball-handling visit: running and the simultaneous ball-free finale are other activities, not extra rolling attempts. That fits an introduction, not sustained ball practice. Check the first cycle; if slower, shorten the roll/return route or narrow the goal openly. Don't count waiting poses as ball attempts. A request for more repetitions needs a different duration or approved staffing/layout, not faster feeds.

**Observe/help:** watch whether the child faces the lane and moves behind the roll. The cue names a place to move toward. If the roll passes, check the feed, demonstrate slower/closer, then observe the retry; don't label the child. If a cleared assistant joins, retain this whole-group plan. Before assigning a role, use 17–19 for a stopped demonstration/teach-back, leaving 19–23 for route practice; no unbriefed independent station.

After all pickups: allow two adult minutes for remaining gear and three for a self-review of one success, one wait/setup problem, and one adjustment. Increase those allowances when needed.
