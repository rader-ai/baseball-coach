# 60-minute coach-pitch practice

## Field card

**Ten players around ages 7–8; two approved adults.** Goals: try contact and practice controlling a ground ball before returning it to a ready adult. This rehearses part of fielding to first, not the full game situation. Verify league equipment, coverage/care rules, and current conditions.

**Gear/places:** tee, suitable bat, fitted required helmets, soft practice balls, appropriate sound protective screen for pitching, cones, water. A takes five at hitting; B takes five at rolling. Verify separated sectors, waiting/return routes, and errant-ball paths. Only the called hitter holds a bat; A and waiting children stay outside full swing/ball paths. No child catcher/live fielder at hitting. For feeds, A uses a correctly positioned screen outside full bat reach, checked against league policy/equipment instructions. Without that setup, use the tee; soft balls do not guarantee protection. Never use unprotected hardball front feeds.

**FREEZE:** hold balls, park bat, close lanes before retrieval/movement. Adults keep groups in view; A coordinates restart after count/readiness. B rolls to one ready child, who controls it, looks at B's ready glove, and throws or rolls back in the same lane; no separate distant target/return loop.

| Time | Activity and adults |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Intake, places, stop/retrieval demonstration. |
| 5–10 | A leads ready-pose movements; B supports spacing. Walking/seated option. |
| 10–13 | Both show their action/cue, then place groups. |
| 13–24 | A: gentle protected feeds or tee, “Watch the ball.” B: roll/control/return, “Look, then throw.” Easier: closer straight roll and roll back; harder: gentle side roll, not more speed. Include changes/retrieval; final minute secures/counts. |
| 24–28 | Water, count, supervised group swap. |
| 28–39 | Same adults, new groups; brief demonstration, attempts, stopped collection within block. |
| 39–43 | Water; secure bats/screen as needed without losing coverage. Verify a rolling lane in each existing sector. |
| 43–53 | A/B each lead five at roll/control/return in those independent lanes. First minute shows positions; final minute closes/collects. Repeat the skill rather than add baserunning. |
| 53–57 | **Final fun game: Mystery Play.** All gear secured. A mimes fielding; everyone copies ready/stop poses and points to the named base from personal spaces. B supports. Seated options, optional child suggestions, no elimination. |
| 57–60 | Brief optional reflection, one observed appreciation, optional cheer/wave, begin pickup. |

Total: 60 minutes. Water is available throughout. Stop for pain/fatigue, distress, unsafe space/weather or lost coverage. Use the established care process. If sectors cannot stay independent, run one combined activity and explicitly reduce the skill goal; don't add an assumed lane. If either adult is absent, do not run the two-station plan: retain guardian care until approved feasible coverage/briefing, or postpone. All authorized pickups remain supervised after minute 60.

## Detachable assistant card — B

**Job:** one roll/return lane, five children; later A copies this in the cleared sector. Adult feeds and receives from the same end; waiting children stay outside the lane in view. **Purpose/cue:** control before return; “Look, then throw” directs attention to the ready target. **Watch/retry:** ball controlled? Target checked? Show a slow roll, stop, look, return; retry closer or rolled back. Add a gentle side roll only when ready. One ball per lane, no surprise throws or separate child catcher. **Stop/ask:** secure ball, retain group, call A for care, pain, distress, unsafe path or uncertainty. Collection/change only after lane closure and coordinated count/restart.

## Preparation notes — before players

T−15 to T−8: A verifies hitting sector and screen/feeder setup (otherwise tee); B checks gear, return lane and water. T−8 to T−3: both demonstrate cues, safer retries, places, stop/collection and teach back. T−3 to T0: conditions/coverage and intake readiness. These are familiar-setup allowances; increase adult time if needed. Guardians retain care until accepted handoff.

**Opportunity decision:** budget about eight usable minutes in each child's first fielding block and seven in the second after demonstrations, collection, and margin. At a conservative 30-second complete cycle, fifteen minutes supports around six attempts each, about 2½ minutes between the same child's turn starts within a continuously running visit—not across the whole practice. The direct return and second exposure replace a longer empty-target/retrieval route and unrelated running block. If first cycles are slower, reduce distance/route complexity or focus on control-and-roll-return; don't call a few tries sustained throwing practice. Hitting has one eleven-minute block each: with around eight usable minutes and a 60–75-second short turn, expect one turn plus some retries per child, not many hits. Tee use can simplify the task without rushing it.

Illustrative inter-visit gaps: assume attempt windows 14:00–22:00, 29:00–37:00, and 44:00–51:00, 30-second cycles, and the same turn order on return. Children who hit first have about 7½–10 minutes between their last first-visit ball attempt and first returning attempt; children who field first have about 22½–25 minutes. The longer gap includes supervised hitting, water, and transitions, not continuous idle waiting. Changed order or extra pauses may lengthen it; these are planning assumptions, not measured rates.

For A, a missed feed/contact prompts a paused bat, check of setup, one demonstration and comfortable tee choice, not stacked corrections. No fixed number of swings is a game rule here.

Late intake uses 24–28; extra instruction replaces 28–29 attempts. Late approved-helper teach-back can replace 28–30, while the stopped group stays under permitted coverage. If coverage is not established, keep guardians responsible or end; don't invent solo permission. After all pickups, allow two adult minutes for remaining gear and three for one success, one wait/setup problem, and one adjustment; increase as needed.
