# End-season awards: inclusive 15-minute ceremony

Original sample for ten children. Two approved adults: A presents, B helps with access, keepsakes, and handoff. Confirm local safeguarding arrangements, accessible space, and preferred name pronunciations. No purchases or photographs are required.

| Time | Run of show |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | A welcomes families: “Thank you for being part of our season. You can join in, wave, or simply listen.” |
| 2–4 | A names two real team moments: [observed moment], [observed moment]. Thank helpers without ranking contributions. |
| 4–9 | Ten recognitions, about 30 seconds each including movement. Use the same format and equal-value keepsake, if any, for everyone. |
| 9–12 | Optional reflections: invite up to three brief contributions, about one minute each including transition. Passing is welcome; use any spare time for quiet thanks. |
| 12–13 | Optional team word, wave, or quiet acknowledgment. No required touching or chant. |
| 13–15 | Thank families; adults coordinate equipment and supervised pickup. |

Total: 15 minutes. Recognition wording: “Thank you, [preferred name], for [specific observed action]. We're glad you were part of this team.” Example actions only when true: inviting a teammate into line, returning equipment, or trying again after a missed ball. If no observation is available, use the same warm team thank-you for that child; don't invent one.

Avoid competitive superlatives, teasing labels, or public comparisons. Offer a seated or quiet presentation and recognition later for absent children. If more than ten children attend, recalculate the recognition block and total duration. Any optional photo requires appropriate permission and an easy opt-out; publishing it is a separate decision.

For two quiet recognitions, reserve two of the ten 30-second slots at the beginning or end of the 4–9 block. With caregiver agreement, A speaks quietly to each child at the edge of the visible gathering while B supervises the group; don't announce their preference or require them to come forward. The remaining eight slots still total four minutes. If a family prefers recognition later, agree that separately. Supervision continues after the program until all required guardian handoffs are complete.
