**Subject: Welcome to the River Pebbles — our first practice**

Hi families,

I'm [coach], and I'm looking forward to helping our children enjoy baseball. Our priorities are feeling welcome, trying new skills, and learning to be teammates. No previous experience is needed.

Our first practice is [confirmed date/time] at [confirmed field]. Bring a glove if available, water, and [league-required clothing/gear]. We have [confirmed shared equipment]; please check with us before buying a bat or helmet.

We'll build from the basics with short activities, one simple cue at a time, and water breaks. We plan to finish practice with a fun game and a positive goodbye, with safety taking priority. Children can ask for a pause or an easier version. Please tell me privately through [approved adult channel] if an adjustment would help your child participate.

Attendance updates go to [channel]. Weather decisions come from [league contact/channel]. Pickup follows [confirmed league process]. Please keep private health or contact information out of group replies.

If you'd like to help with equipment or coaching, contact [adult contact]. On-field volunteers must complete the league's approval requirements. From the sideline, please cheer effort and kindness and let coaches give the instructions.

Thank you,
[Coach]

**A few common questions**

- **What if my child is nervous?** Watching first or starting with a simpler activity is welcome. Let's talk privately about what helps.
- **Will my child try different positions?** We'll plan varied opportunities within local rules, readiness, and equipment requirements.
- **Do we need extra lessons or expensive gear?** Neither is an expectation of this beginner team. Check shared gear and league requirements before purchasing.
- **What should we do at home?** If your child wants to, try a few gentle rolls with a caregiver in a clear space. It's optional.
- **What if we miss a session?** Let us know through the attendance channel; we'll help your child rejoin the next activity.
- **How should I raise a concern?** Contact me privately away from active play. Raise an immediate safety concern right away with the responsible adult.
